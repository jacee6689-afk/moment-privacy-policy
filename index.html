Moment Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Moment app handles information when you use it.

Audio recordings and storage

Moment saves recordings in its private app storage on your device. Moment does not provide an account or an app-operated server for uploading your recordings. The app does not include a sharing or export feature; recordings can be played or deleted inside Moment. Deleting a recording in Moment removes its local app copy. Device backups or other copies managed outside Moment may be subject to your device settings.

Microphone, motion, and voice commands

Microphone access is used to make recordings and, when enabled, listen for your voice command. Motion sensor readings are used on your device to detect the motion trigger. Your saved custom voice command is kept in the app's settings on your device.

When the voice trigger is on, audio is processed by the speech-recognition service provided by your operating system. Depending on your device, settings, and service, speech may be processed on your device or sent to the service provider. Moment does not control that provider's processing; review the provider's privacy information. Moment uses the recognition result to check for your command and start recording.

Device authentication

Moment asks your operating system to authenticate you before opening protected app content. Moment does not receive or store your PIN, passcode, fingerprint, or face data.

Advertising and consent

When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. Google processes information collected through its SDK under its own terms and privacy policy. Moment uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required and to provide privacy choices. You can use the advertising privacy-options control in Moment when it is available.

Your choices

You can manage microphone and motion permissions in your device settings, stop a voice trigger in Moment, and delete recordings in the app. You can also review your advertising privacy choices through Moment's in-app privacy-options control when provided.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
